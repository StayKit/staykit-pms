Questions, answered

Frequently asked.

Is StayKit really free if I self-host? +

Yes. AGPL-3.0. No “open-core” tricks, no feature flags. Every feature on the cloud version is in the open-source repo. If you can run Docker, you can run StayKit.

Why do you not have a channel manager / OTA sync? +

Because it's a separate, much harder problem that 70% of homestay owners don't actually need. If most of your bookings are direct, phone, WhatsApp, or Instagram — StayKit is for you. If 50%+ come from OTAs, look at Djubo or Hostaway.

How is the AI assistant different from a chatbot? +

A chatbot answers questions. StayKit's AI takes actions — creates bookings, sends payment links, processes refunds — with your permission, fully logged, fully reversible.

Can the AI mess things up? +

Every AI action requires a permission scope you grant explicitly. Destructive actions (refunds, cancellations) need separate scopes. Every action is in the audit log. You can revoke AI access in one click.

What about my guest data — is it safe? +

Your data lives on Indian servers (cloud tier) or your own servers (self-host). Guest ID documents are encrypted at rest. We follow DPDP Act 2023 + DPDP Rules 2025 to the letter.

Do I need GST registration to use StayKit? +

No. If your turnover is under ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in HP, Uttarakhand, Northeast), you can use StayKit without GSTIN. We'll keep your invoices ready for when you cross the threshold.

Can I switch from Djubo / Qloapps / spreadsheet? +

Yes. We import from CSV, Google Sheets, and most PMS exports. Free migration help on Standard and Dedicated plans.

What if StayKit shuts down? +

You export your data anytime, in standard formats (JSON, CSV, SQL). Or you keep using the open-source version — it'll keep working without us.

Is there a free trial? +

Yes — 14 days, no credit card required. After that, ₹149/month or self-host for free.

What about WhatsApp Business API? +

We support it via MSG91, Gupshup, and Interakt. You'll need an approved Meta Business account and pre-approved templates. We help with the setup.
